This page shows who is taking part in planning the cooperative, so members can see what skills, passions, land and produce the group already has between them. It is the first task of the suggested route: meet everyone interested and record what each person does, where, and what training they have [MS].

Nobody listed here is a member of a cooperative yet. Membership starts only when the cooperative is registered.

## How people are listed

- Everyone taking part has agreed to be listed with their contact details, so members can reach each other directly. People who join later agree when they join. See [[how-to-take-part|How to Take Part]].
- Each person has their own page with their full name, town, phone number, email if they give one, their skills, what they are passionate about, and what they could offer the cooperative. Growing is one skill among many: cooking, building, selling, guiding, teaching, bookkeeping and driving all count.
- No ID numbers, street addresses or bank details.
- Anyone can ask for their contact details, or their whole listing, to be removed, and they are removed straight away.

## Skills

Each person's page lists one or more of these skills. The list is a starting point: when someone has a skill that is not here, send it to the WhatsApp group and the editor adds it, with its own tab in the table.

| Skill | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Growing | Vegetables, fruit, herbs, seedlings, gardens |
| Livestock | Chickens, pigs, goats, sheep, cattle, bees |
| Cooking | Meals, catering, cooking for groups |
| Baking | Bread, cakes, rusks, baked goods to sell |
| Preserving | Jams, pickles, drying, bottling, other food that keeps |
| Crafts | Sewing, weaving, beadwork, pottery, woodwork |
| Art | Painting, music, performance, design |
| Building | Construction, repairs, plumbing, electrical work, fencing |
| Selling | Market stalls, shopkeeping, selling to restaurants and guesthouses |
| Marketing | Social media, photography, writing, promotion |
| Guiding | Tours, hikes, farm visits, hosting visitors |
| Heritage | Knowledge of the valley's history, stories and traditions |
| Teaching | Training, mentoring, working with young people |
| Bookkeeping | Accounts, budgets, keeping track of money |
| Admin | Organising, minutes, forms, paperwork |
| Driving | Transport, deliveries, a vehicle the group could use |
