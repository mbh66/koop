The group meets once a week to plan the cooperative. Anyone who is interested may come.

## When and where

- **Day and time:** once a week, agreed on 5 November 2026. The day and time are still to be agreed.
- **Place:** to be agreed at the first meeting.
- **Online:** to be agreed, for those who cannot come in person.

## Next meeting

Sunday 8 November 2026, 14:00. Place to be shared in the WhatsApp group. Bring your own idea or business plan on paper. The agenda will go up here before the meeting. Send agenda items to the WhatsApp group.

## Notes from past meetings

Each meeting has its own page, named by date. The newest is at the top.

- [[meetings/2026-11-05-meeting|Meeting of 5 November 2026]]

## How the notes are kept

- The editor posts the agenda before the meeting and the notes within a few days after it.
- The notes record who came, what was discussed, what was decided, and who said they would do what by when.
- A decision taken at a meeting is also added to [[decisions/index|Decisions]].
- Anyone who was at the meeting can ask for a correction in the WhatsApp group. Corrections are made and dated on the page.
