The group meets once a week to plan the co-operative. Anyone who is interested may come.

## When and where

- **Day and time:** to be agreed at the first meeting.
- **Place:** to be agreed at the first meeting.
- **Online:** to be agreed, for those who cannot come in person.

## Next meeting

No meeting has been set yet. The agenda will go up here a few days before each meeting. Send agenda items to the WhatsApp group.

## Notes from past meetings

Each meeting has its own page, named by date. The newest is at the top.

None yet.

## How the notes are kept

- The editor posts the agenda before the meeting and the notes within a few days after it.
- The notes record who came (by first name, and only for those who agree), what was discussed, what was decided, and who said they would do what by when.
- A decision taken at a meeting is also added to [[decisions/index|Decisions]].
- Anyone who was at the meeting can ask for a correction in the WhatsApp group. Corrections are made and dated on the page.
