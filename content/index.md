*Koop* is Afrikaans for co-operative. This site is where people in the Valley of Grace who have said they want to join a growers' co-operative plan it together, between and during their weekly meetings.

The idea behind it is set out on the Ward 2 Development Forum's site: [Valley of Grace Agri-Heritage Co-operative](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/concepts/valley-of-grace-agri-heritage-co-operative). Growers in the valley would own one business together. Each keeps their own farm or garden, and the co-operative does what is hard for one small grower to do alone: buying in bulk, processing, selling under one name, hosting visitors, and training.

> [!warning] Nothing here is agreed yet
> The co-operative does not exist yet. It has not been registered, and the idea is still to be tabled at the Forum's November 2026 meeting [MS]. Pages on this site record what the group is discussing. A decision counts only once the group has taken it at a meeting and it appears on [[decisions/index|Decisions]].

## How this site works

The group talks in its WhatsApp group and meets once a week. One person, the editor, keeps this site up to date on everyone's behalf. If you want something added or changed, send it to the [WhatsApp group](https://chat.whatsapp.com/IQvXYVLd58hFLyQ7vTA8zT?mode=gi_t). See [[how-to-take-part|How to Take Part]].
