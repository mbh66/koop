These are the questions the group needs to answer before the cooperative can be formed. They start from the list in the [cooperative idea](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/concepts/valley-of-grace-agri-heritage-co-operative) on the Forum's site. When the group answers one, the answer goes on [[decisions/index|Decisions]] and the question is marked here with the date.

## Who and where

- Who else would join the growers in Genadendal, and from which towns?
- Whether to form one cooperative, or smaller ones that later join a larger one.
- How to agree on whether someone interested in joining can join.

## Land and water

- Which land members would farm, and on what terms. The land of Genadendal, Bereaville, Voorstekraal, Boschmanskloof and Madiba Park is part of the unresolved TRANCRAA process [MS]. The [Genadendal Transformasie Komitee](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/civic/governance/genadendal-transformasie-komitee) has not been asked for its view.
- Where members' water would come from.

## Money

- What the cooperative would cost to register, run, and report on. Nothing has been costed yet [MS].
- What members would pay to join, if anything, and what they would contribute.
- Which grants and support programs would apply, and whether every member would qualify.

## Structure

- Whether the cooperative would also need a separate trading company, or a nonprofit for training, to keep grant money and business income apart.
- How the board would be chosen before the first AGM.

## Working with others

- How the cooperative would work beside existing food projects and markets, such as [Valley Food Gardens](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/economic/farmers-land-water/valley-food-gardens) and [Greyton Village Market](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/economic/business/greyton-village-market), so that it adds to their work. Neither has been asked yet.
- Whether the Overberg's district plan is in place, and what it says about farming.

## Adding a question

Send it to the WhatsApp group, or raise it at a meeting. The editor adds it here under the heading it fits.
