A working group is two or more people who take on one piece of the planning between weekly meetings and report back. The full group sets each one up at a meeting, and the working group brings its proposals back to the full group to decide.

No working group has been set up yet. The groups below follow the activities and next steps in the [co-operative idea](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/concepts/valley-of-grace-agri-heritage-co-operative). They are suggestions for the group to accept, change or drop.

## Suggested groups

### Growing

Plan planting across member farms and gardens so there is produce all year, and work out what seed, compost and tools could be bought in bulk.

### Processing

Find out what surplus fruit and vegetables could become (jams, dried goods and other products that keep), and what kitchen, equipment and food-safety certificates that would need.

### Selling

Look at a shared farm shop, a shared name, and supplying local restaurants, guesthouses and markets. Work out how this fits beside existing markets such as [Greyton Village Market](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/economic/business/greyton-village-market).

### Visitors and heritage

Plan farm visits, heritage walks and meals made from members' produce. Heritage walks would share the valley's history on terms the community sets.

### Learning

Find out what training members and young people need in growing, food handling, guiding and running a small business, and who could provide it.

### Constitution and business plan

Draft the constitution and business plan the co-operative needs before it can register. The suggested route expects at least five founding members to do this [MS]. See [[registration/index|The Route to Registration]].

## Each working group's page

Once the full group sets up a working group, it gets its own page in this folder with: who is in it, what it was asked to do, by when, and what it has found. The page is listed here.
