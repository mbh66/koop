Koop is a shared notebook for the people planning the Valley of Grace Agri-Heritage Co-operative. It supports the group's weekly meetings: the agenda goes up before each meeting, the notes go up after it, and decisions are recorded in one place so nobody has to search back through WhatsApp.

## Who it is for

Anyone in the valley who has said they are interested in joining the co-operative: small-scale and emerging growers, commercial farmers, community gardens, and people with skills in processing, selling, guiding or training. Partners who are helping the group can read it too.

## Who keeps it

One editor makes every change, working from what members send to the group's WhatsApp group and from what is said at meetings. The editor does not decide anything on the group's behalf. When the editor is unsure how to record something, the question goes back to the group. See [[how-to-take-part|How to Take Part]].

## How it relates to the Forum

The idea for the co-operative came from a group of growers in Genadendal and was written up on the Ward 2 Development Forum's site [MS]. The Forum keeps that page and helps groups like this one find each other and connect with the municipality. It does not join or run any co-operative [MS]. This site is the group's own working space. Once the co-operative is formed, its members decide what happens to it.

## Who can see it

The site is public. Anyone with the address can read it, though it asks search engines to leave it out of their results. Write and post only what you are happy for anyone to read.

- Names of members appear only with that person's agreement. See [[members/index|Who Is Taking Part]].
- No phone numbers, ID numbers, home addresses or bank details ever go on a page.
- Prices, quotes and other people's business details stay in the WhatsApp group until the people concerned agree to share them.

## What the letters in square brackets mean

Facts on this site are followed by a tag that shows where they come from.

- **[IC] Independently Corroborated.** Confirmed by at least two sources that do not depend on each other, or by an official record.
- **[MS] Mission-Sourced.** Comes from the people putting the co-operative forward, in their own documents or statements. It has not been checked against other sources.
- **[TBV] To Be Verified.** Seems likely, but nobody has confirmed it yet.

Statements without a tag are proposals. They describe what the co-operative could become.
