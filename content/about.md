*Koop* is a shared notebook for the people planning the Genadendal Cooperative. It supports the group's weekly meetings: the agenda goes up before each meeting, the notes go up after it, and decisions are recorded in one place so nobody has to search back through WhatsApp.

## Who it's for

Anyone in the valley who has said they are interested in joining the cooperative: small-scale and emerging growers, artists and artisans, commercial farmers, community gardens, and people with skills in processing, selling, guiding or training. Partners who are helping the group can read it too.

## Who maintains it

One editor, Michael Haupt, makes every change, working from what members send to the group's WhatsApp group and from what is said at meetings. The Cooperative will decide a new editor as soon as it is ready. The editor does not decide anything on the group's behalf. When the editor is unsure how to record something, the question goes back to the group. See [[how-to-take-part|How to Take Part]].

## How it relates to the DevForum

The idea for the cooperative came from a group of growers in Genadendal and was written up on the site of the DevForum (Ward 2 Development Forum) [MS]. The DevForum keeps that page and helps groups like this one find each other and connect with the municipality. It does not join or run any cooperative [MS]. This site is the group's own working space. Once the cooperative is formed, its members decide what happens to it.

## Who can see it

The site is public. Anyone with the address can read it, though it asks search engines to leave it out of their results. Write and post only what you are happy for anyone to read.

- Everyone taking part has agreed that their name and contact details are listed, so members can reach each other directly. See [[members/index|Who Is Taking Part]].
- ID numbers, street addresses and bank details never go on a page.
- Prices, quotes and the details of people outside the group stay in the WhatsApp group until the people concerned agree to share them.
- Anyone can ask for their details to be removed, and they are removed straight away.

## What the letters in square brackets mean

Facts on this site are followed by a tag that shows where they come from.

- **[IC] Independently Corroborated.** Confirmed by at least two sources that do not depend on each other, or by an official record, for example, meeting minutes or a municipality announcement.
- **[MS] Mission-Sourced.** Comes from the people putting the cooperative forward, in their own documents or statements. It has not been checked against other sources.
- **[TBV] To Be Verified.** Seems likely, but nobody has confirmed it yet.

Statements without a tag are proposals. They describe what the cooperative *could* become.
